Upanishad Saaram

This section is dedicated to the Vedic principles and teachings contained in the Upanishads. The Upanishads are a divine master, containing principles and teachings of life. This section includes maximum information on all available texts, collated from across the world. I am working to include all 108 principal Upanishads here.
