Upanishad Saaram

This section is entirely dedicated to the Vedic principles and teachings contained in the Upanishads. One translation of the term Upanishad is: "Sitting near the enlightened," a person who has reached enlightenment and teaches his spiritual practice is referred to as a guru. The Upanishads are in a way a divine master, containing principles and teachings of life. This is now entirely revamped to include maximum information on all available texts - collated from across the world. All relevant copyrights are duly acknowledged. I am trying my best to make available the 108 principal Upanishads in this section. With God's grace, it shall be achieved very soon.
