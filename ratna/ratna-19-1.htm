Translation of "Prasnottara Ratna Maalika" - Verse-19

Verse 19 - Meaning:
Q: Who is praised even by the celestial ones (the devas) ?
A: That one who is full of kindness and mercy. (dayaa-pradhaanaaya)
Q: What should cause fright in one ?
A: The sight (or even thought) of the forest of samsaara , if one is a man of chastened understanding.
