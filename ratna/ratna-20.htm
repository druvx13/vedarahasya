Translation of "Prasnottara Ratna Maalika" - Verse-20

Verse 20 - Meaning:
Q: To whom do all living beings submit and obey ?
A: To that one whose speak is truthful and full of mercy, and who has humility.
Q: Where should one establish oneself ?
A: In the path that is righteous and that yields benefits here and in the hereafter (during and after this worldly life).
