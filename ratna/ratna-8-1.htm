Translation of "Prasnottara Ratna Maalika" - Verse-8

Verse 8 - Meaning:
Q: What should be considered as nectarine words by the ears ? (karnam - ears; amrutam - something nectarine or sweetest)
A: The advice of holy elders (sad upadesah)
Q: What makes one respectable?
A: A character that makes one abhor begging for favors (vishaya).
