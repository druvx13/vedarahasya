Translation of "Prasnottara Ratna Maalika" - Verse-28

Verse 28 - Meaning:
Q: What (kaa) attracts (manohara) the mind of the learned ? (vidvan)
A: Literary compositions without a blemish (sat-kavitaa) and the damsel of masterly intelligence (bodha vanitaa ca)
Q: Who (kam) can save himself from calamities ? (na sprsati vipattih)
A: That one who follows the advice of wise men (pravrddha vacana) and who exercises control over the senses.(anuvartinam daantam)
